MILESTONE 3 SUMMARY
===================

Status: Complete (October 28, 2026). Started October 26, 2026.

Objective
---------
Let students choose which events they see: by time frame and by category,
on the map and in the list of events that can't be pinned.

Done when: a user can pick a time frame and categories and see only those
events, on the map and in the list. This is true. (The goal was changed on
October 26: originally "pick a day and time", with pins colored by
category.)

What Was Accomplished
---------------------

1. Categories, food and registration (categorize.py)
   - New step in the pipeline, run after locate.py:
     gt_calendar_rss.py -> locate.py -> categorize.py -> map.html
   - Food: from GT's "Free food" Extras tag, food words in the name, or
     phrases like "lunch will be served" in the write-up. Called "Food",
     not "Free food": whether it's free is for the student to check
   - Registration required: register / RSVP / sign up / tickets in the
     related links, location, fee or write-up ("Registrar" doesn't count)
   - Each result has a reason (e.g. "name: pizza"), shown when hovering
     the tag on the map
   - Also sorts events into our own categories (social, professional,
     sports, academic, arts, other). The map no longer shows these (see Key
     Decisions), but the rules are kept for possible later use
   - The RSS reader now also stores each event's full write-up ("details")
     for these clues

2. Filters (map.html)
   - Time frame: Today / Tomorrow / Next 7 days / Select a date. Events
     that have already ended are hidden; multi-day events show on every
     day they cover
   - Category checkboxes: Food plus GT's 10 category names exactly as on
     its RSS feeds page, each with a count for the chosen time frame; all
     ticked at the start, with All / None buttons. An event shows if it has
     any ticked category
   - The pins and the "Not on the map" panel both follow both filters

3. Event cards (map pop-ups and the panel)
   - Name (linked to GT's event page), time range (e.g. "Wed, Oct 28,
     11:00 AM – 4:30 PM"), location, category tags, Food and "Registration
     required" tags
   - Location text is shown even when it can't be matched to a building,
     so students can find the place themselves
   - Descriptions removed from the panel to keep it short on phones

4. Layout and look
   - GT gold (#B3A369) pins with navy (#003057) border and number, and a
     gold category bar
   - "Not on the map" panel on the left of the map (below it on phones)
   - Panel sections: Online, Off campus, Location not matched/found, No
     location listed. Vague in-person places ("Georgia Tech Campus",
     "Off-site", "Various") now go under "Location not matched/found"
     instead of Online
   - The page always loads the latest events.json, so rerunning the
     pipeline shows up on a normal reload

Results (October 28, 2026)
--------------------------
- 225 upcoming events; every one has at least one of GT's 10 feed
  categories, so none are hidden by the category filter
- Events per GT category: Other/Miscellaneous 60, Training/Workshop 60,
  Career/Professional development 46, Arts and Performance 34,
  Seminar/Lecture/Colloquium 32, Student sponsored 13, Conference/Symposium
  8, Sports/Athletics 6, Institute/Academic/Religious 0, Special event 0
- Food: 13 events (10 from GT's own "Free food" tag)
- Registration required: 91 events. A hand check on October 26 (89 of 219
  flagged then) found 1 clearly wrong and 2 doubtful (~3%), and no misses
- Locations: 89 pinned, 1 off campus, 34 unmatched, 72 online, 29 with no
  location. Match rate for in-person events: 73%, down from 83% on October
  26, partly because "Georgia Tech Campus" now counts as an unmatched
  in-person place rather than online

Key Decisions
-------------
- Rules, not an LLM, for categories, food, registration and locations.
  The rules' error rate is small and measurable; an LLM would add cost and
  randomness.
- GT's own category names for the checkboxes (October 28). Students
  already know the names on GT's feeds page, so our own groupings would
  confuse them. Food stays, since GT has no food category. GT's extra tags
  that have no feed ("Performance", "Talk", "Free", ...) are left out.
  Empty or broken feeds still get a checkbox, so the list doesn't change
  when GT fixes them.
- No category colors on the map: all pins look the same, and checkboxes
  choose what shows.
- No time-of-day picker: a day or a range of days is enough.
- gt_calendar_scraper.py (the old page scraper) removed from GitHub; the
  owner keeps a local copy.

Key Files
---------
- scraper/gt_calendar_rss.py: reads the RSS feeds, writes events.json
- locate.py: matches events to buildings, prints the match rate
- categorize.py: food, registration and (unused) category rules
- data/gt_buildings.json: buildings, keywords, coordinates, sources
- map.html: the map page with filters
